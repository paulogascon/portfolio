01 — How the studio works

You bring the substance.
I handle the finish.

Paulo Creative Studio is a one-person design and production desk. Every project starts from your own content: your outline, notes, draft, data, photos or footage. My job is to make it clear, consistent and good-looking, and to deliver files you can keep editing.

What I do
  • Design slide decks and templates from your outline or script.
  • Lay out guides, handbooks, brochures and other PDFs.
  • Format Word documents: styles, headings, tables of contents, captions and references.
  • Proofread your writing with tracked changes, so you approve every edit.
  • Rebuild resumes and cover letters from your real history.
  • Edit short-form video from your footage, with captions and clean audio.
What I don't do
  • Write theses, research papers, essays or any graded coursework for someone else to submit.
  • Take quizzes, exams or online classes.
  • Invent data, sources, quotes, reviews or credentials.
  • Rewrite your argument while calling it proofreading.

Students are welcome for formatting and proofreading of work they wrote themselves. If your school limits outside editing, please check with your instructor first.

Good to know

Every delivery includes the editable source file (PPTX, DOCX or project file) plus a PDF or MP4 export. You own the final files.

02 — Services

Five services, one standard.

Each service uses the same habits: a shared style sheet for your project, consistent spacing and type, accessible contrast, and files named so you can find them later.

01

Presentation decks

16:9 PowerPoint decks for pitches, trainings, reports and events. Built on proper slide masters so new slides stay on-brand.

Includes: theme and master layouts · icons and charts from your data · Morph and subtle transitions · speaker-notes cleanup · PPTX and PDF export
02

Studio PDFs and guides

Handbooks, onboarding kits, checklists, brochures, event programs and lead-magnet guides with editorial layouts.

Includes: cover and interior layout · tables, callouts and diagrams · print-ready and screen PDFs · editable source
03

Word formatting, APA styling and proofreading

Your document set up the way Word is meant to work, so headings, numbering and the table of contents update themselves.

Includes: heading styles and automatic TOC · page numbers, headers and section breaks · APA 7 or house-style formatting · tracked-changes proofreading for grammar, spelling, punctuation and consistency
04

Resumes and cover letters

Clean, ATS-friendly resumes rebuilt from your actual experience, plus a matching cover letter layout.

Includes: one- or two-page resume · matching cover letter template · DOCX and PDF
05

Video edits

Short-form reels and simple long-form edits from your footage: cuts, captions, music and loudness-balanced audio.

Includes: auto-transcribed captions checked by hand · kinetic titles · audio mastered to −14 LUFS for social platforms · vertical and horizontal exports

04 — Process

From brief to final files
in five steps.

1

Brief

Send what you need, your deadline and your content through the inquiry form, Messenger or email.

2

Quote

You get a scope, a price and a delivery date. Work starts when you confirm.

3

First draft

A complete first version, not a mood board, so feedback is concrete.

4

Revisions

Comments on the PDF or a short list in chat. Rounds are set by your package.

5

Delivery

Source files plus exports, named and organised in one shared folder.

What to send with your brief

  • Your content. Outline, draft, notes or footage.
  • Purpose and audience. Who will read or watch it, and where.
  • Brand assets. Logo, colours and fonts, if you have them.
  • Rules to follow. Style guide, APA edition or page limits.
  • Deadline. The date you need the final files.
