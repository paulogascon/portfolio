Communication rhythm

Daily

Check-in

Three lines at the start of the overlap window: done, doing today, blocked or waiting.

Weekly

Report

One page on the agreed day: completed work, open items, decisions needed and next week's plan.

Monthly

Review

A short call to adjust priorities, retire tasks, add SOPs and review access.

Daily check-in format

Done: what was finished since the last check-in. Today: what is planned. Blocked: anything waiting on the client, with the decision needed.

03 — Weekly report template

One page. Five answers.

The weekly report should take the client two minutes to read and leave nothing they have to chase.

Weekly reportWeek of ________
Completed
In progress
Waiting on you
Decisions needed
Next week
Hours logged

Writing tips

  • Lead each line with the outcome, not the activity.
  • Link to the file or thread instead of describing it.
  • Put anything that needs a reply under "Decisions needed".

Where it lives

  • A shared doc or sheet, one tab or page per week.
  • Sent at the same time every week.
  • Kept in the client's drive, not the assistant's.

04 — First week plan

A calm first week.

Day 1

Access

Accept invitations, set up a client browser profile, fill the access log, test every login.

Day 2

Inbox rules

Agree labels or folders, priority senders and reply templates. Triage with the client watching.

Day 3

Calendar

Confirm booking rules, buffers and meeting types. Clean up the next two weeks.

Day 4

First SOP

Write down one repeated task step by step and get it approved.

Day 5

Report

Send the first weekly report and agree what changes next week.

Offboarding checklist

  • Open tasks listed with status and next step.
  • SOPs and templates saved in the client's drive.
  • Files moved out of any personal storage.
  • Sessions signed out on every device.
  • Access removal confirmed against the access log.
  • Final report and handover call completed.
