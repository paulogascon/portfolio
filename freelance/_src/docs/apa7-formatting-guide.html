01 — Page setup

Set these once,
before you format anything.

Most APA problems come from fixing pages by hand. Set the page, the font and Word's built-in styles first, and the rest of the paper follows.

Margins
1 inch (2.54 cm) on every side.
Font
Any accessible font used consistently. APA lists 11-pt Calibri, 11-pt Arial, 10-pt Lucida Sans Unicode, 12-pt Times New Roman, 11-pt Georgia and 10-pt Computer Modern as examples.
Line spacing
Double-space the whole paper, including the title page, block quotations and reference list. Table and figure content may be single-, 1.5- or double-spaced.
Alignment
Left-aligned with a ragged right edge. Do not justify text or insert manual hyphens.
Paragraphs
Indent the first line 0.5 inch using Word's paragraph settings, not the Tab key. No extra blank lines between paragraphs.
Page numbers
Top right corner of every page, starting with 1 on the title page.
Running head
Not required for student papers unless your instructor asks for one. Professional papers use one.
Order of pages
Title page, abstract (only if assigned), text, references, footnotes, tables, figures, appendices. Tables and figures may also be placed in the text.
Word shortcut

Modify the Normal style once (font, double spacing, 0 pt before and after, 0.5-inch first-line indent) and every paragraph inherits it.

02 — Title page and headings

The student title page.

1
Title of the Paper in Bold Title Case
 
Author Name
Department, University
Course Number: Course Name
Instructor Name
Assignment Due Date
  1. Page number top right, starting at 1.
  2. Title three or four lines down from the top margin, centered, bold, in title case.
  3. One blank double-spaced line between the title and the author name.
  4. Author name, then affiliation: department and university.
  5. Course number and name, as listed in your school's materials.
  6. Instructor name, in the form your instructor prefers.
  7. Due date in the format used in your country, for example November 25, 2026.

Five heading levels

Repeat the paper's title in bold at the top of the first text page. Do not add a heading called "Introduction".

Quotations

  • Add a page or paragraph number to direct quotes: (Author, 2024, p. 12) or (Author, 2024, para. 4).
  • Quotes of 40 words or more become a block quotation: new line, indented 0.5 inch, double-spaced, no quotation marks.
  • Use "et al." from the first citation when a work has three or more authors.

Reference-list patterns

Patterns show the order of elements. Italics, capitals and punctuation are part of the format.

The reference page

Start on a new page. Label it References, centered and bold. Order entries alphabetically by the first author's surname, double-space them and use a 0.5-inch hanging indent.

04 — Final check

Twelve checks before
you submit.

  • Margins are 1 inch on every side.
  • One font and size used throughout the text.
  • Double spacing everywhere, with no extra space between paragraphs.
  • Page numbers top right on every page.
  • Title page has all seven student elements.
  • Headings follow the level formats in order.
  • Every citation in the text has a reference entry, and the reverse.
  • Author names and years match between text and references.
  • References are alphabetical with a hanging indent.
  • DOIs are written as https://doi.org/ links.
  • Tables and figures have a bold number and italic title above them.
  • Your instructor's rules have been checked, since they override this guide.

Tables and figures

Number them in the order they are mentioned. Put the number in bold (Table 1), the title on the next line in italic title case, and any note below the table or figure.
