Studio policy

Design, formatting and editing. Never ghostwriting.

What I do

  • Design decks and templates from your outline or script
  • Lay out guides, handbooks, brochures and other PDFs
  • Format Word documents and apply APA 7 or your house style
  • Proofread your writing with tracked changes you approve
  • Rebuild resumes from your real experience
  • Edit short-form video from your footage

What I don't do

  • Write theses, research papers, essays or graded coursework for someone to submit
  • Take quizzes, exams or online classes
  • Invent data, sources, reviews or credentials
  • Rewrite your argument and call it proofreading

Students are welcome for formatting and proofreading of work they wrote themselves. If your school limits outside editing, check with your instructor first.

06 · Questions

          
Good to know.

        

        

          
Will you write my essay, thesis or research paper?
No. The studio designs, formats and proofreads content you wrote yourself. It does not write graded coursework, take exams or produce work for someone else to submit as their own.

          
Can students hire the studio?
Yes, for formatting your own paper to APA 7 or your school's guide, a proofread with tracked changes, a resume, or a deck for a non-graded event or organisation. If your school limits outside editing, please check with your instructor first.

          
What do you need to start?
Your content (outline, draft, data or footage), who it's for, any brand or style rules, and your deadline. The inquiry builder above covers all of it.

          
Do I get editable files?
Always. Decks come as PPTX and PDF, documents as DOCX and PDF, layouts as PDF plus source, and video as MP4 with SRT captions.

          
How do I pay?
GCash for clients in the Philippines. Other methods can be arranged for international clients. The amount, method and timing are written into your quote before any work starts.

          
What hours do you work?
Full-time, 40+ hours a week, from Bacolod City (Philippine time, UTC+8). Working hours can be arranged to overlap with US or UK business hours.
