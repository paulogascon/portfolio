# Image & Visual Assets Attribution Register

This register documents all visual assets, analytical figures, diagrams, and historical imagery integrated across the 15 Freelance Showcase samples, adhering strictly to the **Image Decision & Licensing Protocols** (`09_Antigravity_Image_Decision_Rules.md`).

---

## 1. Summary of Decision Protocol
1. **Academic & Legal Papers (Word/PDF)**: Light visual density. Only substantive, non-decorative figures (empirical charts, conceptual paradigms, statutory flowcharts) with formal captions and source citations.
2. **Guides, Business Plans, & Handbooks**: Rich visual density with full-width cover banners, workflow schematics, and UI mockups.
3. **PowerPoint Presentations**: $\ge 70\%$ visual coverage, combining authentic historical photographs (e.g. Dr. Jose Rizal from Wikimedia Commons) and high-contrast vector illustrations with native Morph transitions.
4. **Ethical Compliance**: Zero fake photos of living individuals, zero fabricated evidence, and zero copyrighted corporate brand logos.
